HOW ORDERING WORKS
-------------------
Step 1 — Browse the catalog on velorastudio.in
Browse Men, Women, Kids, and Gifts categories. Use the filter to find your subcategory.

Step 2 — Tap "Order on WhatsApp"
Each product card has an "Order on WhatsApp" button. Tapping it opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message including the product name, category, and price.

Step 3 — Share your design
Tell us what you want printed — upload a photo, logo, text, or design idea. Our team will suggest adjustments if needed and send you a digital preview before production.

Step 4 — Confirm and pay
Review the preview, confirm the details (size, quantity, colour of garment), and make payment. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and all major payment apps.

Step 5 — We produce and ship
Once confirmed, your order goes into production. You receive a shipping notification with tracking when dispatched.

Step 6 — Delivered to your door
Pan-India delivery. Most orders arrive in 5–7 business days from the date of production confirmation.

CUSTOMISATION
-------------
Every item can be customised with:
- Your photo or design (we accept JPEG, PNG, PDF)
- Custom text or name
- Logos and branding
- Your own artwork

For design help, our team can help with basic layouts and text. We do not offer full graphic design services — if you need a complete design made from scratch, we recommend working with a designer first.

Design tips:
- Use high-resolution images (minimum 300 DPI) for the sharpest print quality
- Photos with clear subjects and good lighting print best
- Simple designs with strong contrast look great on fabric
- Our team reviews every design before printing and will flag any issues

Garment colour: We offer a range of base garment colours (white, black, navy, grey, maroon, and more). Confirm your preferred colour when placing your WhatsApp order.

RETURNS AND REFUNDS
-------------------
Because every item is made to order and personalised specifically for you, we are unable to offer returns or exchanges for change-of-mind.

We do offer a free reprint or refund in the following cases:
- The item arrived damaged or defective
- The print quality is significantly below what was confirmed in the digital preview
- The wrong size or product was delivered

To raise a claim: Contact us on WhatsApp or email admin@velorastudio.in within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos of the issue. We resolve all verified claims within 2–3 working days.

Design errors that were approved by the customer in the preview cannot be claimed.
