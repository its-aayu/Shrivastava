HOW IT WORKS — OUR PROCESS
----------------------------
Step 1 — Upload your artwork
Upload your design file via the dashboard. Accepted formats: PDF, AI, EPS, PSD, TIFF. Minimum 300 DPI. CMYK colour mode.

Step 2 — File check and proofing
Our team reviews your file for bleed, safe zones, resolution, and colour. If there are issues we contact you immediately. A digital proof (PDF) is sent for your approval within 1 working day.

Step 3 — Proof approval
You review and approve the digital proof. No print starts without your approval. If corrections are needed, we revise and resend.

Step 4 — Production
Once approved, your job enters production. You'll receive a confirmation email.

Step 5 — Quality check
Every order is inspected before packing. Colour, cut, and finish are checked against your approved proof.

Step 6 — Dispatch
Your order is packed securely and dispatched via courier. You receive a tracking number by email/SMS.


FILE PREPARATION REQUIREMENTS
-------------------------------
Format: PDF (preferred), AI, EPS, TIFF, PSD
Resolution: 300 DPI minimum (72 DPI screen resolution files are not accepted)
Colour mode: CMYK (not RGB — colours shift during conversion)
Bleed: 3mm on all sides (extend background beyond the cut line)
Safe zone: Keep all text and important elements at least 3mm inside the trim line
Fonts: Embed all fonts or convert to outlines
Colour profiles: ISO Coated v2 or FOGRA39 recommended

Common issues to avoid:
- RGB files (colours will shift, especially blues and purples)
- Missing bleed (white edges appear after cutting)
- Text too close to the trim edge (may be cut off)
- Low resolution images (will appear blurry when printed)
- Using Canva — export as PDF Print (not PDF Standard) with bleed turned on

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
----------------------------------

Q: Can I see a physical sample before placing a large order?
A: Yes, sample packs are available on request for business cards and packaging. Contact admin@aayuprinting.in to request samples. Samples may be chargeable for custom sizes.

Q: What is the minimum order quantity?
A: Minimums vary by product: 50 for business cards and packaging, 100 for labels, 25 for brochures, 10 for DTG apparel, 24 for screen print. There is no minimum for large format posters.

Q: Can I rush my order?
A: Yes, rush orders are available on most products. Contact us before placing the order to confirm rush slot availability. Rush orders typically add a 20–30% surcharge.

Q: Do you offer design services?
A: We offer basic design assistance and template setup. For full design work, we recommend working with a designer. Our team can suggest corrections to your file or help with minor layout adjustments.

Q: Do you print for customers outside India?
A: Our primary market is India. For international orders, please contact us to discuss shipping costs and feasibility.

Q: Can I place a very large or corporate order?
A: Yes, we handle large corporate orders for branding, events, and campaigns. Contact admin@aayuprinting.in for volume pricing and dedicated support.

Q: What happens if I don't have a design ready?
A: You can place a quote request first and work on the design while we prepare pricing. Production does not start until your design file is uploaded and approved.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order from the Orders section of your dashboard.

Q: Can I print on eco-friendly materials?
A: Yes, we offer FSC-certified paper, recycled kraft stock, water-based laminates, and soy-based inks on selected products. Mention your eco preference when requesting a quote.

Q: What is Soft Touch lamination?
A: Soft Touch is a premium lamination finish that gives printed materials a velvety, suede-like texture. It looks matte but feels noticeably different from standard matte lamination. It is popular for luxury business cards and high-end packaging.

Q: What is Spot UV?
A: Spot UV is a high-gloss coating applied to specific areas of a printed piece (logo, pattern, or text). It creates a contrast between the matte background and the shiny coated areas, giving a premium, tactile effect.

Q: What is Foil Stamping?
A: Foil stamping applies a metallic or holographic foil to selected areas using heat and pressure. Available in gold, silver, rose gold, copper, and holographic. It cannot be applied on rush orders due to the additional processing step.

Q: What GSM should I choose for business cards?
A: 350 GSM is the standard for quality business cards. For a thicker, more premium feel, choose 400 GSM or above. Letterpress cards are typically 600+ GSM.

Q: What is the difference between CMYK and RGB?
A: RGB is used for screens; CMYK is used for print. All files must be in CMYK mode. If you submit an RGB file, colours will shift during conversion — especially blues, purples, and neon colours. Always convert to CMYK in your design software before exporting.

Q: Do you accept Canva files?
A: We accept Canva exports, but they must be exported as PDF Print (not PDF Standard) with the bleed option turned on (3mm). Standard Canva PDFs often lack bleed and embed fonts incorrectly.

Q: What is a bleed?
A: A bleed is an extra 3mm border extended beyond the final cut size of your design. It prevents white edges from appearing after the paper is cut. Always set up your document with a 3mm bleed on all sides.
